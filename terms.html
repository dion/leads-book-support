Free version and Leads Book Pro

The free version has no ads. It holds a limited number of active leads (New, Warm and Hot; currently 25) and Lists (currently 3), on your phone and in the cloud; Cold, Converted and Dud leads are unlimited. Leads Book Pro removes those limits and adds message templates, pipeline stats, sync of Lists and the activity log, and a home-screen widget. The current limits, features and prices are shown in the app before you buy.

Pro is tied to the Google Play account you buy it with, so it works without a Leads Book account. When you are signed in, it also covers that Leads Book account's cloud sync. A purchase can be linked to one Leads Book account only. Pro bought in Leads Book applies only to Leads Book, not to Contacts Book, and the other way round.
