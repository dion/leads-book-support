Privacy policy

Leads Book (com.jellopops.leadsbookmobile) by Dions Apps · Effective November 10, 2026

Leads Book helps you track leads, follow-ups and appointments. This policy explains what the app stores, what leaves your device, and how to delete it.

Data stored on your device

The app keeps what you enter in private storage on your device: each lead's name, phone number, email address, Instagram and WhatsApp handles, status, notes, follow-up and appointment dates, reminders, schedule history and timestamps; your lists; an activity log; and your profile (name, phone, role and an optional photo). This data stays on your device unless you sign in to cloud sync.

Your device contacts

With your permission, the app reads your device contacts so you can import a person as a lead. It only reads them; it never changes or adds contacts. It lists the accounts on your device only so you can choose which contacts to show. Contacts are never uploaded, except as the lead fields listed below for leads you create, when cloud sync is on.

Photos and notifications

If you choose a profile photo, the app reads that one photo from your library and keeps it on your device; it is never uploaded. Follow-up reminders are scheduled as notifications on your device; no notification service receives your lead details.

Optional cloud sync

The app works without an account. If you create one or sign in:

  • Your email address and password are handled by Amazon Cognito to create and secure your account. Your email is used for sign-in and to send confirmation and password reset codes.
  • For each lead, the app uploads the name, phone number, email address, Instagram and WhatsApp handles, status, notes, follow-up date, reminder, schedule history and timestamps to our server on Amazon Web Services (US East region). Lists, the activity log, your profile and your photo are not uploaded.
  • All data is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections and is accessible only with your signed-in account.
  • Server logs record technical request details (such as time, route, status and your account ID) for 14 days for troubleshooting. They do not contain your lead details.
  • Leads Book shares accounts with our Contacts Book app: the same email and password sign in to both, and leads synced from one appear in the other.

What we don't do

  • No ads, and no analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell your data. Your leads, notes and contacts are never shared with third parties. Amazon Web Services processes data only as our hosting provider.

Retention and deletion

Data on your device stays until you delete it or uninstall the app. Cloud data stays until you delete it. Deleting a lead removes it from the cloud; a small deletion marker without its details is kept for up to 90 days so your other devices can sync the deletion.

You can delete your account and all cloud data at any time from Settings → Delete account in the app, or by following the steps on the account deletion page.

Children

Leads Book is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
