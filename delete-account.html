Delete your Leads Book account

Leads Book by Dions Apps

Leads Book and Contacts Book share accounts. Deleting your account deletes it, and its cloud data, for both apps.

Delete in the app

  1. Open Leads Book and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account at the bottom.
  3. Enter your password and tap Delete. Your account and all of its cloud data are deleted immediately.

Delete without the app

Email dyang@dionyang.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Leads Book account". We will delete your account and cloud data within 7 days and reply to confirm.

Delete some data but keep your account

Delete any lead in the app (open the lead, then tap the trash icon). It is removed from the cloud on the next sync; a small deletion marker with no contact details is kept for up to 90 days so your other devices remove it too. To have all cloud data erased while keeping your account, email dyang@dionyang.com from your sign-in address and we will do it within 7 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: email address and password.
  • Every lead synced to the cloud: names, phone numbers, emails, Instagram and WhatsApp handles, statuses, notes, follow-up dates, reminders, schedule history and timestamps.

What is kept

  • Leads already on your phone stay on your phone, and the app keeps working without an account. Delete them in the app or uninstall the app to remove them.
  • Server request logs, which hold your account ID but no lead details, expire automatically within 14 days.
